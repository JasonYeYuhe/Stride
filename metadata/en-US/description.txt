Great things aren't built in a day — they're built one day at a time. Stride is the habit tracker designed to help you show up consistently, track your progress beautifully, and build the life you want, one streak at a time.

Whether you're starting a morning routine, learning a new language, hitting the gym, or drinking more water, Stride makes it simple, satisfying, and motivating to stay on track across all your Apple devices.

TRACK YOUR HABITS, YOUR WAY

Create habits in seconds and check them off with a single tap. Stride keeps things beautifully simple so you spend less time in the app and more time doing what matters. Set daily goals, customize icons and colors, and organize habits into the perfect dashboard for your life.

STREAKS THAT KEEP YOU GOING

There's nothing quite like watching your streak grow. Stride tracks your consecutive days automatically and gives you that satisfying feeling of momentum. Miss a day? No guilt — just pick back up and keep moving forward. Your best streak is always saved so you can challenge yourself to beat it.

STATISTICS & HEATMAPS

Dive deep into your progress with detailed statistics and beautiful GitHub-style heatmaps. See your completion rates over weeks, months, and years. Understand your patterns — which days you're strongest, where you tend to slip, and how far you've come since day one.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS

Keep your habits front and center with 5 different widget styles for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. See today's progress at a glance, track your top streaks, or get a quick overview of your weekly heatmap — all without opening the app. Available in small, medium, and large sizes.

SMART REMINDERS

Never forget a habit again. Set custom push notification reminders for each habit at exactly the right time. Morning meditation at 7 AM, evening journal at 9 PM — Stride nudges you gently so your habits become automatic.

SYNC ACROSS ALL YOUR DEVICES

Start your morning check-in on your iPhone, review your stats on your iPad, and track your evening habits on your Mac. With Stride account sync, your data follows you everywhere. Sign up once and everything stays perfectly in sync.
