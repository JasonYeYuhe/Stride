Nothing worth building happens in a day. Stride is the habit tracker that helps you show up, see your progress, and keep the streak alive — one day at a time.

A morning routine, the gym, or drinking more water: Stride keeps you on track on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

TRACK YOUR HABITS, YOUR WAY

Create habits in seconds — from scratch or from a ready-made template — and check off a yes-or-no habit with a single tap. Pick an emoji and a color for each one, and order your list your way.

Not every habit is a simple yes or no. Measurable habits let you set a daily target and a unit, so “Drink Water — 8 glasses” or “Run — 5 km” tracks the amount, not just the checkmark.

And not every habit is daily. Choose every day, a set number of times per week, or specific days — Monday, Wednesday, and Friday only, if that’s your gym schedule. Hit a weekly goal on whichever days suit you; that streak counts weeks, not days.

STREAKS THAT KEEP YOU GOING

Stride counts every streak automatically, so you can watch your momentum build. On a day your habit isn’t scheduled, it’s a rest day — it never breaks your streak. Miss a day? No guilt — just pick back up. Any streak can be shared as an image.

STATISTICS & HEATMAP

The Activity Heatmap is a color-coded grid of your last 12 weeks. You also get your current and best streak, a strength score that recovers from a missed day, your 30-day completion rate, and how you do by weekday. For a specific-days habit, that rate counts only the days it was due. Forgot to check in? Fix past days in your history.

WIDGETS ON IPHONE AND IPAD

Small, medium, and large widgets on the Home Screen (extra large on iPad), plus circular, inline, and rectangular ones on the Lock Screen. See today’s progress without opening the app, and check off a habit right from the medium and large widgets.

REMINDERS FOR EVERY HABIT

Give every habit its own reminder, at the time you choose: meditation at 7 AM, a journal at 9 PM. Mark it done or snooze it right from the notification.

SYNC ACROSS YOUR DEVICES

Check in on your iPhone, review your stats on your iPad, finish the day on your Mac. With a free Stride account, your data follows you.

STRIDE PRO

Almost everything is free: unlimited and measurable habits, flexible schedules, widgets, reminders, the heatmap and statistics, templates, streak sharing, CSV or JSON export, and sync.

Stride Pro adds three things:

• Habit Groups — organize habits into collapsible groups
• Advanced Analytics — Insights, the 8-week Trend chart, the Strength chart and your top 5 streaks
• Weekly Review — any week at a glance: what improved and what is at risk

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Your habit data is yours. Stride keeps it on your device unless you sign in to sync; when you do, it goes to Stride’s own server and is used only to keep your own devices in step — never sold, never shared for advertising. Stride collects anonymous crash and diagnostic reports to fix bugs; they contain no habit content and are not linked to your identity.

START TODAY

You don’t need a Monday or a new year. You need today. Download Stride and check off one habit.
