Stride

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Stride does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your habit data stays on your device.

Overview

Stride is a habit tracking app for iOS and macOS. We believe your personal habits are your business and no one else's. This privacy policy explains how Stride handles your data.

Data Storage

All habit data you create in Stride is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data is never sent to any external server.

Stride uses Apple's App Groups to share your habit data between the main app and the home screen widget. This data sharing happens entirely on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

Stride does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or tracking data
  • Location data
  • Health or fitness data

No Account Required

Stride does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no registration process, and we do not store any credentials.

Notifications

Stride may send you optional push notifications to remind you about your habits. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and are not routed through any external server. You can enable or disable notifications at any time through the app or your device settings.

Subscriptions & Payments

Stride offers optional subscriptions through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. For information about how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party SDKs

Stride does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app uses only Apple-provided frameworks.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all of your Stride data at any time by deleting the app. No data remains on any server because none was ever sent to one.

Children's Privacy

Stride does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, no special provisions are necessary.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
