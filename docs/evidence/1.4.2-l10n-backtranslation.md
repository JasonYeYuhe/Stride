# 1.4.2 localization lens: trial and renewal terms (W12, 2026-11-18)

RELEASE-1.4.2.md W12 asks for a translation lens that back-translates the trial and renewal terms
in ja and ko, and for zh to be reviewed with the owner. The terms live in two places:

- **The store descriptions** (`metadata/<locale>/description.txt`, purchase section). O23's clause
  is new in all six; the renewal sentences are 1.4.1's, unchanged.
- **docs/terms.html** is English only (W9 added the trial paragraph and Lifetime), so it has no
  ja or ko text to back-translate.

The back-translations below are literal, by the agent (not a native speaker), and are meant to
show that the legal meaning survived: when the charge happens, the 24-hour cancellation window,
and that an unused trial ends on subscribing.

## The English source (O23, D16)

> If a free trial is offered, you are charged when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours
> before it ends. Any unused part of a trial ends when you subscribe.

Three facts: (1) conditional ("if … offered"), so it is true with no live offer; (2) the charge
falls at the trial's end unless cancelled ≥ 24 h before that end; (3) the unused remainder ends on
purchase of a subscription (Apple's standard "forfeited when the user purchases a subscription").

## ja

**Trial clause (new):**

> 無料トライアルが提供される場合、トライアル終了の24時間前までに解約しない限り、トライアル終了時に料金が請求されます。サブスクリプションを購入すると、無料トライアルの未使用期間はその時点で終了します。

Back-translation: "If a free trial is provided, unless you cancel by 24 hours before the end of
the trial, the fee is charged at the end of the trial. When you purchase a subscription, the
unused period of the free trial ends at that point."

- (1) 提供される場合 = "if provided": conditional. OK.
- (2) 24時間前までに解約しない限り … 終了時に請求 = "unless cancelled by 24 h before the end,
  charged at the end". "〜前までに" is the usual Japanese for "at least N before", and it is the
  same construction as the existing renewal sentence. OK.
- (3) 購入すると … 未使用期間はその時点で終了 = "on purchase, the unused period ends then". OK.

**Renewal (1.4.1, unchanged):**

> お支払いは、購入確定時にお客様のApple IDアカウントに請求されます。自動更新サブスクリプションは、現在の期間終了の24時間前までに自動更新をオフにしない限り自動的に更新され、更新料金は現在の期間終了前の24時間以内にアカウントに請求されます。

Back-translation: "Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when the purchase is confirmed. An
auto-renewable subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off by 24 hours
before the end of the current period, and the renewal fee is charged to the account within the 24
hours before the current period ends." Matches the en-US paragraph.

## ko

**Trial clause (new):**

> 무료 체험이 제공되는 경우, 체험이 끝나기 최소 24시간 전에 취소하지 않으면 체험이 끝날 때 요금이 청구됩니다. 구독을 구매하면 무료 체험 중 사용하지 않은 기간은 그 즉시 종료됩니다.

Back-translation: "If a free trial is provided, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the
trial ends, a charge is billed when the trial ends. If you purchase a subscription, the period
of the free trial you have not used ends immediately."

- (1) 제공되는 경우 = "if provided". OK.
- (2) 최소 24시간 전에 취소하지 않으면 … 끝날 때 청구 = "unless cancelled at least 24 h before,
  billed at the end". Same "최소 24시간 전" as the existing renewal sentence. OK.
- (3) 구독을 구매하면 … 그 즉시 종료 = "on purchasing a subscription, ends at once". "그 즉시"
  (at once) is slightly stronger than the English "when"; the meaning (the end coincides with the
  purchase) is the same. OK.

**Renewal (1.4.1, unchanged):**

> 구독의 경우, 구매 확인 시 Apple ID 계정으로 요금이 청구됩니다. 현재 기간이 종료되기 최소 24시간 전에 자동 갱신을 해제하지 않으면 구독은 자동으로 갱신되며, 갱신 요금은 현재 기간이 종료되기 24시간 이내에 계정에 청구됩니다.

Back-translation: "For subscriptions, the fee is charged to the Apple ID account on purchase
confirmation. Unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends,
the subscription renews automatically, and the renewal fee is charged to the account within 24
hours before the current period ends." Matches the en-US paragraph.

## es-ES (checked, not required)

> Si se ofrece una prueba gratuita, se te cobrará cuando termine, a menos que la canceles al menos 24 horas antes de que termine. La parte no utilizada de una prueba termina cuando te suscribes.

"If a free trial is offered, you will be charged when it ends, unless you cancel it at least 24
hours before it ends. The unused part of a trial ends when you subscribe." Matches.

## zh-Hans and zh-Hant: for the owner's review

The owner reads zh natively; these are the choices an owner should confirm or reword.

**Trial clause:**

- zh-Hans: 如提供免费试用，除非你在试用结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则将在试用结束时扣款。购买订阅后，免费试用中尚未使用的部分随即终止。
- zh-Hant: 如提供免費試用，除非你在試用結束前至少 24 小時取消，否則將於試用結束時收費。購買訂閱後，免費試用中尚未使用的部分隨即終止。

To check:
1. **扣款 vs 收費.** zh-Hans says 扣款 to match the paragraph's own 扣款续期; zh-Hant says 收費
   to match its 收取續訂費用. Fine if the two are meant to differ by region.
2. **随即终止 / 隨即終止** ("ends right then") is formal. Alternatives: 即告终止, or 将自动结束.
3. **如提供** is written register; 如果提供 reads more casually but matches the rest less.
4. The clause sits at the end of the auto-renew paragraph (zh-Hans: the 自动续期订阅 paragraph,
   before 终身版; zh-Hant: inside its one purchase paragraph, before 購買後可隨時前往…).

**Keywords** (each comma term compared whole with the name and subtitle):

- zh-Hans dropped 习惯打卡 (it is the name's own phrase, 每日习惯打卡), 连续打卡 and 每日计划 (打卡 and
  每日 are in the name or subtitle); it now uses 连续记录 (the app's word for a streak) and 计划.
  Is 连续记录 a phrase people search, or should 连续打卡 stay despite the overlap?
- zh-Hant dropped 習慣打卡 and 連續打卡 (習慣 is in the name, 打卡 in the subtitle). 習慣打卡 is not
  literally in zh-Hant's name (每日習慣追蹤): keep it if you think the whole phrase ranks.
- 无限 / 無限 lead for "unlimited habits", as O25 asks; 不限数量 is the more natural phrase but
  costs two more characters.
- Added from the description's own examples: 早起, 健身/運動, 喝水, 阅读/閱讀, 冥想, 作息.

**What's New:**

- 标记 (zh-Hans) / 標記 (zh-Hant) for the Mac's notification badges: check against macOS's own
  notification settings in each script.
- zh-Hant uses 輔助使用 (Apple Taiwan's word for Accessibility); zh-Hans uses 辅助功能.
- "10 分或以上" in both: 以上 alone already includes 10 in most readings.
